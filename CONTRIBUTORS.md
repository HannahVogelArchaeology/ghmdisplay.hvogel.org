# Contributors

**Disability Heritage: Archaeology with Difference** is a collaborative public museum exhibition at Macquarie University's Gale History Museum and online at <https://ghmdisplay.hvogel.org>.

## Curator and Content Owner

- **Hannah Vogel** — Curator, School of Humanities, Macquarie University (exhibition concept, archaeological scholarship, curatorial prose, object metadata, audio descriptions, transcripts, and video introductions).

## Technical and Accessibility Lead

- **Dr Brian Ballsun-Stanton** — Technical and Accessibility Lead, Macquarie University (website synthesis architecture, accessibility engineering, continuous integration, and long-term maintenance).
