    <h2 id="website-accessibility">Website Accessibility</h2>
    <p>Accessibility has shaped every stage of this exhibition website, reflecting the exhibition's commitment to disability heritage as shared public culture. The site is designed and tested to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, and all colour themes are engineered to achieve Level AAA contrast ratios of at least 7:1 for body text, links, captions, and controls.</p>

    <h3>Customisable display settings</h3>
    <p>Select the <strong>Accessibility</strong> button in the navigation bar on any desktop or mobile page to tailor how the site looks and reads. Your choices are stored only on your own device without cookies, and you can restore the defaults at any time using <strong>Reset all display settings</strong>.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Reading font:</strong> Self-hosted <strong>Lexend</strong> is the default typeface, selected to reduce visual crowding. You can switch between four self-hosted typefaces: <strong>Lexend</strong>, <strong>Atkinson Hyperlegible</strong> (created by the Braille Institute for low-vision legibility), <strong>OpenDyslexic</strong>, and <strong>Gentium</strong> (a serif option).</li>
      <li><strong>Text spacing (anti-crowding):</strong> Choose <strong>Default</strong>, <strong>Relaxed</strong>, or <strong>Wide</strong> spacing to expand line height, paragraph gaps, letter spacing, and word spacing up to WCAG 1.4.12 thresholds. When Lexend is active, the spacing dial also steps automatically to wider Lexend letter-spacing grades.</li>
      <li><strong>Text size:</strong> Adjust root text size between <strong>80% and 200%</strong> using relative (<code>rem</code>) scaling so columns, controls, and tables reflow cleanly without clipping or horizontal scrolling.</li>
      <li><strong>Display themes and system preferences:</strong> Switch between <strong>Auto</strong> (matching your device setting), warm archival-paper <strong>Light</strong>, warm charcoal <strong>Dark</strong>, and stripped-back <strong>High Contrast</strong>. The site also honours Windows High Contrast (<code>forced-colors: active</code>), increased system contrast (<code>prefers-contrast: more</code>), and reduced motion (<code>prefers-reduced-motion: reduce</code>).</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Multimodal exhibition media and privacy</h3>
    <p>Exhibition media is designed so visitors can engage with each artefact through sight, sound, Auslan (Australian Sign Language), or text:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>The spoken Video Introduction and the Auslan Introduction include closed captions and expandable full transcripts.</li>
      <li>Every object includes an audio description player with play and pause, volume, playback speed, a seek bar, <strong>−10s</strong> and <strong>+10s</strong> skip buttons, and a full written transcript.</li>
      <li>Object photographs open in an enlarged viewer equipped with zoom controls and single-click pan buttons alongside drag and pinch gestures.</li>
      <li>Where interactive 3D scans are available, high-resolution multi-angle photographs, descriptive alt text, and audio descriptions are always provided alongside them so no visitor depends on a 3D viewer to explore an object.</li>
      <li>Aggregate page visits are measured using cookieless analytics; the site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.</li>
    </ul>

  <section class="accessibility__section" aria-labelledby="keyboard-and-screen-reader-tools">
    <h2 id="keyboard-and-screen-reader-tools">Keyboard and Screen Reader Tools</h2>
    <p>Every interactive control on this website—including navigation links, the Accessibility panel, gallery thumbnails, audio description controls, enlarged photo buttons, and 3D viewers—sits in the standard browser tab order. Move through controls with <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd>, activate links or buttons with <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>, and close any open menu, panel, search box, or viewer with <kbd>Esc</kbd>. Closing a dialogue returns keyboard focus to the button that opened it.</p>

    <p>Pages use semantic landmarks (banner, primary navigation, main content, search, and footer), an ordered heading hierarchy, descriptive alt text on all photographs and gallery thumbnails, and concise polite status announcements for dynamic updates. The first tab stop on every page is a <strong>Skip to main content</strong> link.</p>

    <h3>Site Search and keyboard shortcuts</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Search exhibition:</strong> Open Site Search from the <strong>Search</strong> button in the header, by pressing <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on macOS), or by pressing <kbd>/</kbd> outside text fields and dialogues. Search matches object numbers, titles, accession numbers, materials, shelves, and site pages; use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>Tab</kbd> to move through results and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to jump directly to an object or page.</li>
      <li><strong>Section stepper and back to top:</strong> Long pages include buttons to step between headings or return to the top navigation bar. Outside form fields and open dialogues, <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>↑</kbd> also returns focus to the top of the page.</li>
      <li><strong>Visual Keyboard Guide:</strong> Turn on <strong>Keyboard Guide</strong> inside the Accessibility panel to display visual key badges beside interactive controls and a key legend at the top of the page without changing tab order.</li>
      <li><strong>Enlarged photo viewer:</strong> Select any photograph or its Enlarge button to open the full-screen viewer. Use the on-screen buttons or <kbd>+</kbd>, <kbd>−</kbd>, and <kbd>0</kbd> to zoom and fit, drag or use the single-click pan buttons and arrow keys to move around a zoomed photograph, and press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

    <h2 id="on-campus-visitor-information">On-Campus Visitor Information</h2>
    <p>This exhibition is displayed at the <a href="https://www.mq.edu.au/faculty-of-arts/about/gale-history-museum">Gale History Museum</a> from <strong>10 November 2026 to 11 March 2027</strong>. The Museum is open <strong>10am to 4pm, Monday to Friday</strong> (closed on public holidays) and is located on Macquarie University's Wallumattagal Campus, North Ryde, on the Ground floor, Arts Precinct, 25C Wally's Walk. Please refer to grid reference P-12 on the <a href="https://www.mq.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0016/108142/Campus-Map.pdf">Macquarie University Campus Map</a>. Parking and public transport information is available via the Museum's <a href="https://www.mq.edu.au/faculty-of-arts/about/gale-history-museum/visit">Visit page</a>.</p>

    <p>Accessibility features for the on-campus display include:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Tactile 3D-printed models of selected exhibition objects</li>
      <li>A tactile 3D-printed fabrication of the scene depicted on Object 1, Pelike (MU4759)</li>
      <li>Braille and large-print exhibition guides</li>
      <li>An interactive museum iPad touchscreen kiosk and QR codes providing access to this website, including the Auslan introduction video, high-resolution photographs, and audio-visual descriptions.</li>
    </ul>

    <p>If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please report it via the <a href="https://github.com/{{ site.repository }}/issues">GitHub issue tracker</a> or contact the Gale History Museum so we can investigate and address it promptly.</p>
